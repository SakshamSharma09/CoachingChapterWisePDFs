# Class 5 model papers (English, Hindi, EVS, Maths)

10 model papers per subject in the Rajasthan RSCERT "Primary Education Learning Level Evaluation" pattern, written from the supplied sample papers:
80 marks, 2.30 hours, 22 questions (7 one-mark objective, 7 three-mark, 6 six-mark, 2 eight-mark; English: lexical 7 + structural 27 + reading 30 + writing 16; Hindi: gadya 22, padya 13, grammar 13, creative 24, paryaveshiya sajagta 8; EVS: seven learning areas; Maths: shape and space 21, number system 9, operations 25, measurement 17, data handling and patterns 8).

Per paper, three PDFs in `<Subject>/Model_Papers/`: `Model_Paper_NN.pdf` (question paper only), `Model_Paper_NN_Answer_Key.pdf` (separate answer key with marking points) and `Model_Paper_NN_Solved.pdf` (questions with answers printed under them, optional). No ruled answer lines are printed, to avoid wasted space.
Questions and passages are original (the textbooks were not available); topics follow the learning areas and chapter themes of the sample papers. Rajasthan-specific EVS facts should be checked by a teacher.
Existing `English/Reading_Writing` is unrelated older material.
