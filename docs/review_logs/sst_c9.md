## Issues found and fixed (9, all fixed)
- Ch01: "(1.5, minus rounding)" stray wording in the firm X/firm Y answer -> "(1.5)". Notes table said needs are "Limited and fairly stable" (not in NCERT) -> "Essential for living and working" (patched in the ws file; the shared source in rn_work was not touched).
- Ch03: The Chamoli 2021 case was presented as a glacier/GLOF event, but NCERT only says a devastating flood struck and asks students to find the causes. Question (i) reworded (hazard studied in the chapter that can cause such a sudden flood); two notes lines (key numbers table, case note) corrected to say the textbook leaves causes open.
- Ch04: Carbon-footprint question (Aman) was impossible against NCERT: only the transport question has a 4-point "very high" option; other categories max out at 3. Question rewritten (scores 2,2,2,3; maximum 4+3+3+3 = 13; total 9) and answer rewritten.
- Ch05: (a) Hard Chinese-dynasties answer attributed civil services and paper currency to the Han dynasty; NCERT says only that silk became a major trade item under the Han (giving the Silk Route its name) and that China was first with paper currency and public-exam civil services -> fixed. (b) Comparison answer called Mesopotamian rulers "priest-kings"; NCERT does not say so -> reworded to kings in palaces with a powerful temple priesthood.
- Ch06: Sabha/samiti/vidhata answer swapped the functions (NCERT: sabha = smaller, elite, mainly judicial; samiti = larger, broader population, policy decisions; vidhata = popular gathering, warfare) -> corrected. North/south administrative-unit matching question made unambiguous (adhishthana = district in north; vithi = group of villages in north; options annotated with their level).
- Ch13: Bhavisha/Dhruv question omitted the ₹1,000 annual saving that the NCERT figures (₹1,99,635 and ₹94,460) rest on; question now states it. (I verified the figures: FV of ₹1,000 a year at 7% is 1,99,635 after 40 payments and 94,460 after 30 payments.)
