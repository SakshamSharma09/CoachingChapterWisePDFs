# Review log: english_c9_c10a (Class 9 English c9e_ch01-08, Class 10 English prose c10e_ch01-18)

Method: every worksheet (WS) and notes (CH) block was read against the NCERT PDF text of the lesson (extracted with pymupdf from Kaveri units and First Flight / Footprints Without Feet). Each factual, comprehension, extract, vocabulary and grammar answer was checked against the text; MCQs were checked for one correct option; level tags and marks were checked; notes were checked for invented detail. All 26 scripts were re-run and the validator passes for each (48-56 questions in the range, levels >= 10, notes >= 1400 words). The first worksheet page and a notes page were rendered (Class 9 Ch02 worksheet, Class 10 Ch16 notes): no clipping.

Limit: the Kaveri units contain only the units' own texts. "Quality", "The Last Leaf" and the poems are included in the PDFs and were checked; the authorship of "Gifts of Grace" is given as anonymous in the PDF.
