## Where our earlier papers differed
* 40 questions (20+8×2+8×3+4×5) with no case-study section, no Assertion-Reason, no figures, no map/source questions.
* Recall/formula-level questions; the school papers are ~50% scenario/competency based with Indian real-life contexts and multi-step sub-questions.
* Wrong internal-choice pattern; wrong mark splits for Science/SST/Class 8 (60 marks, 2.5 h).
* Chapter-by-chapter banks instead of full mid-term-style papers.

## What the new papers do
* Exact section structure, question counts, mark splits and choice counts of each sample; answer keys with worked solutions/marking schemes.
* SVG figures (coordinate planes, circles, solids, force/ray/circuit diagrams, cells/tissues, India map).
* Scenario-first stems, A-R, statement/match/chronology items, source- and case-based questions.
